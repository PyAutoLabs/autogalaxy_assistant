# Sources: stellar halos and galaxy outskirts

Everything below ~26 mag/arcsec² is where structural fitting and sky subtraction stop being
separable problems. These papers establish that the faint outskirts carry real signal, and
how easily that signal is destroyed by the reduction.

## Zibetti 2004 — stacked halos around edge-on disks

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Zibetti2004`
**Reference:** arXiv:astro-ph/0309623, MNRAS 347, 556 — "Haloes around edge-on disc galaxies
in the Sloan Digital Sky Survey"
**Concepts:** [[sky-subtraction-and-photometry]], [[disk-galaxy-structure]]

**Supports:**
- Stacking thousands of SDSS images detects a diffuse stellar halo around edge-on disk
  galaxies far below the single-image surface-brightness limit.

**Use when:**
- Arguing that halo light exists below the depth of a typical image, and that stacking is
  one way to reach it.

**Do not use for:**
- Individual-galaxy halo measurements.

## D'Souza 2014 — parametrizing stellar haloes

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `DSouza2014`
**Reference:** arXiv:1404.2123, MNRAS 443, 1433, doi:10.1093/mnras/stu1194 — "Parametrizing
the Stellar Haloes of Galaxies"
**Concepts:** [[sky-subtraction-and-photometry]], [[photometric-structural-fitting]]

**Supports:**
- A parametric description of the stellar-halo component in stacked profiles, and how its
  amplitude scales with galaxy stellar mass.

**Use when:**
- Justifying an extra outer component in a fit, or interpreting excess light beyond the main
  profile.

**Do not use for:**
- Claiming an individual galaxy's outer excess is an accreted halo — the measurement is
  statistical.

## Merritt 2016 — Dragonfly diffuse halos

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Merritt2016`
**Reference:** arXiv:1606.08847, ApJ 830, 62 — "The Dragonfly Nearby Galaxies Survey. I.
Substantial Variation in the Diffuse Stellar Halos around Spiral Galaxies"
**Concepts:** [[sky-subtraction-and-photometry]], [[disk-galaxy-structure]]

**Supports:**
- Diffuse stellar halo fractions vary substantially from galaxy to galaxy at fixed mass.

**Use when:**
- Cautioning against assuming a universal outer profile.

**Do not use for:**
- High-redshift systems.

## Trujillo 2016 — the low-surface-brightness frontier

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Trujillo2016`
**Reference:** arXiv:1510.04696, ApJ 823, 123, doi:10.3847/0004-637X/823/2/123 — "Beyond 31
mag/arcsec^2: the low surface brightness frontier with the largest optical telescopes"
**Concepts:** [[sky-subtraction-and-photometry]], [[point-spread-function]]

**Supports:**
- The practical limits of deep imaging are set by systematics — flat fielding, scattered
  light and the extended PSF wings — not by photon noise.

**Use when:**
- Explaining why "go deeper" does not automatically mean "measure the outskirts better".

**Do not use for:**
- A specific galaxy's halo mass.

## Huang 2018 — individual stellar halos with HSC

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Huang2018`
**Reference:** arXiv:1707.01904, MNRAS 475, 3348, doi:10.1093/mnras/stx3200 — "Individual
Stellar Haloes of Massive Galaxies Measured to 100 kpc at 0.3 < z < 0.5 Using Hyper
Suprime-Cam"
**Concepts:** [[early-type-galaxy-structure]], [[sky-subtraction-and-photometry]]

**Supports:**
- Stellar-mass profiles of individual massive galaxies measured out to 100 kpc with deep
  wide-field imaging.
- Outer profiles vary at fixed total mass, so aperture choice materially changes a measured
  stellar mass.

**Use when:**
- Justifying a large mask/fitting region for a massive early-type galaxy.

**Do not use for:**
- Low-mass galaxies.

## Duc 2015 — deep imaging of early-type galaxies

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Duc2015`
**Reference:** arXiv:1410.0981, MNRAS 446, 120 — "The ATLAS3D Project – XXIX. The New Look of
Early-type Galaxies and Surrounding Fields Disclosed by Extremely Deep Optical Images"
**Concepts:** [[early-type-galaxy-structure]], [[sky-subtraction-and-photometry]]

**Supports:**
- Deep optical imaging reveals shells, streams and tidal features around a large fraction of
  nearby early-type galaxies that shallower imaging misses entirely.

**Use when:**
- Warning that a smooth model plus residuals may be hiding real, non-axisymmetric structure.

**Do not use for:**
- Quantitative merger rates.

## Harmsen 2017 — GHOSTS halos of Milky Way-mass disks

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Harmsen2017`
**Reference:** arXiv:1611.05448, MNRAS 466, 1491, doi:10.1093/mnras/stw2992 — "Diverse
Stellar Haloes in Nearby Milky Way-Mass Disc Galaxies"
**Concepts:** [[disk-galaxy-structure]]

**Supports:**
- Resolved-star measurements show a wide diversity in halo mass and profile shape among
  Milky-Way-mass disk galaxies.

**Use when:**
- Citing resolved-star evidence rather than integrated-light evidence for halo diversity.

**Do not use for:**
- Distant galaxies, where stars cannot be resolved.

## Buitrago 2017 — stellar halos of massive early-types

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Buitrago2017`
**Reference:** arXiv:1602.01846, MNRAS 466, 4888, doi:10.1093/mnras/stw3382 — "The Cosmic
Assembly of Stellar Haloes in Massive Early-type Galaxies"
**Concepts:** [[early-type-galaxy-structure]]

**Supports:**
- The build-up of the outer stellar envelope in massive early-type galaxies, connecting
  outskirt light to accretion history.

**Use when:**
- Interpreting an extended outer component of a massive early-type as accreted material.

**Do not use for:**
- Disk galaxies.

## Pillepich 2018 — simulated outer stellar mass content

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Pillepich2018`
**Reference:** arXiv:1707.03406, MNRAS 475, 648, doi:10.1093/mnras/stx3112 — "First Results
from the IllustrisTNG Simulations: The Stellar Mass Content of Groups and Clusters of
Galaxies"
**Concepts:** [[early-type-galaxy-structure]], [[stellar-mass-estimates]]

**Supports:**
- Simulation predictions for how stellar mass is distributed between the central galaxy and
  the diffuse component in groups and clusters.

**Use when:**
- Comparing an observed outer profile against a cosmological-simulation prediction.

**Do not use for:**
- An observational claim.
