# Sources: the galaxy-halo connection

Why a structural measurement gets interpreted at all. A size and a stellar mass only become
an evolutionary statement once the galaxy is placed in a dark-matter halo of known mass, and
that mapping comes from abundance matching, clustering and simulations.

## Behroozi 2013 — average star-formation histories in halos

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Behroozi2013`
**Reference:** arXiv:1207.6105, ApJ 770, 57 — "The Average Star Formation Histories of
Galaxies in Dark Matter Halos from z=0-8"
**Concepts:** [[stellar-mass-estimates]]

**Supports:**
- The stellar-mass-to-halo-mass relation and its redshift evolution from z = 0 to 8, derived
  by matching observed stellar-mass functions onto halo populations.

**Use when:**
- Converting a stellar mass into an expected halo mass, or citing the peak efficiency scale.

**Do not use for:**
- An individual galaxy's halo mass.

## Moster 2013 — matching galaxies to halos

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Moster2013`
**Reference:** arXiv:1205.5807, MNRAS 428, 3121 — "Galactic star formation and accretion
histories from matching galaxies to dark matter haloes"
**Concepts:** [[stellar-mass-estimates]]

**Supports:**
- An independent abundance-matching derivation of the stellar-mass-halo-mass relation and
  its evolution.

**Use when:**
- You want a second, independent calibration alongside Behroozi.

**Do not use for:**
- Satellite-specific relations without care.

## Wechsler 2018 — the galaxy-halo connection review

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Wechsler2018`
**Reference:** arXiv:1804.03097, ARA&A 56, 435 — "The Connection between Galaxies and their
Dark Matter Halos"
**Concepts:** [[stellar-mass-estimates]]

**Supports:**
- A review of the methods (abundance matching, halo occupation distributions, empirical
  models) and their systematic differences.

**Use when:**
- One citation is needed for the whole framework.

**Do not use for:**
- A specific numerical relation.

## Kravtsov 2018 — high-mass halo efficiency

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Kravtsov2018`
**Reference:** arXiv:1401.7329, Astronomy Letters 44, 8 — "Stellar mass-halo mass relation and
star formation efficiency in high-mass halos"
**Concepts:** [[stellar-mass-estimates]], [[early-type-galaxy-structure]]

**Supports:**
- At the high-mass end the inferred relation depends strongly on how the central galaxy's
  total stellar mass is measured, including how much of the outer envelope is counted.

**Use when:**
- Connecting the aperture/profile systematics of a structural fit to a cosmological
  inference. This is the cleanest example of that link.

**Do not use for:**
- Low-mass halos.

## Zehavi 2011 — SDSS clustering and the HOD

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Zehavi2011`
**Reference:** arXiv:1005.2413, ApJ 736, 59 — "Galaxy Clustering in the Completed SDSS
Redshift Survey: The Dependence on Color and Luminosity"
**Concepts:** [[sdss]]

**Supports:**
- Galaxy clustering depends on luminosity and colour, and can be interpreted with a halo
  occupation distribution to infer typical host halo masses.

**Use when:**
- Citing the clustering route to halo masses, as distinct from abundance matching.

**Do not use for:**
- Structural parameters.

## Shuntov 2022 — COSMOS2020 stellar-to-halo mass relation

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Shuntov2022`
**Reference:** arXiv:2203.10895, A&A 664, A61 — "COSMOS2020: The cosmic evolution of the
stellar-to-halo mass relation for central and satellite galaxies up to z~5"
**Concepts:** [[cosmos-survey]], [[stellar-mass-estimates]]

**Supports:**
- The stellar-to-halo mass relation measured in the COSMOS field to z ~ 5, separately for
  central and satellite galaxies.

**Use when:**
- Working in COSMOS and needing a halo-mass context for a galaxy's stellar mass.

**Do not use for:**
- Fields with different selection or depth.

## Somerville 2015 — physical models of galaxy formation

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Somerville2015`
**Reference:** arXiv:1412.2712, ARA&A 53, 51 — "Physical Models of Galaxy Formation in a
Cosmological Framework"
**Concepts:** [[stellar-mass-estimates]], [[morphology-classification]]

**Supports:**
- A review of how galaxy formation is modelled within cosmological structure formation,
  including how morphology and structure arise in those models.

**Use when:**
- Framing why structural measurements are a test of galaxy-formation physics.

**Do not use for:**
- A specific numerical prediction.

## Pillepich 2018 — simulated stellar mass in groups and clusters

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Pillepich2018`
**Reference:** arXiv:1707.03406, MNRAS 475, 648, doi:10.1093/mnras/stx3112 — "First Results
from the IllustrisTNG Simulations: The Stellar Mass Content of Groups and Clusters of
Galaxies"
**Concepts:** [[stellar-mass-estimates]], [[early-type-galaxy-structure]]

**Supports:**
- Predictions for how stellar mass divides between a central galaxy and the surrounding
  diffuse component as a function of halo mass.

**Use when:**
- Comparing a measured outer profile or total stellar mass against a simulation.

**Do not use for:**
- An observational claim.
