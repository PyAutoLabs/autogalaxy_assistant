# Sources: disk galaxy structure

The exponential disk, what sits inside it (bulges, pseudo-bulges, bars) and what happens at
its edge (breaks, truncations, anti-truncations). These are the papers behind the modelling
choices a disk-galaxy fit forces you to make.

## Freeman 1970 — the exponential disk

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Freeman1970`
**Reference:** Freeman 1970, ApJ 160, 811 — "On the Disks of Spiral and S0 Galaxies"
(ADS: 1970ApJ...160..811F)
**Concepts:** [[disk-galaxy-structure]], [[sersic-profile]]

**Supports:**
- Galaxy disks are well described by an exponential surface-brightness profile.
- The central surface brightness of disks clusters near a characteristic value ("Freeman's
  law").

**Use when:**
- Justifying an exponential (Sersic n = 1) disk component as the default.

**Do not use for:**
- The outer disk, where the single exponential frequently fails.

## van der Kruit 1981 — three-dimensional disk light distribution

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `vanderKruit1981`
**Reference:** van der Kruit & Searle 1981, A&A 95, 105 — "Surface photometry of edge-on
spiral galaxies. I. A model for the three-dimensional distribution of light in galactic
disks" (ADS: 1981A&A....95..105V)
**Concepts:** [[disk-galaxy-structure]]

**Supports:**
- A three-dimensional model of disk light — radially exponential with a vertical scale
  height — measured from edge-on systems.

**Use when:**
- Fitting an edge-on disk, or motivating a vertical structure parameter.

**Do not use for:**
- Face-on decompositions, where the vertical structure is unconstrained.

## MacArthur 2003 — bulge/disk parameters and secular evolution

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `MacArthur2003`
**Reference:** arXiv:astro-ph/0208404, ApJ 582, 689 — "Structure of Disk-dominated Galaxies.
I. Bulge/Disk Parameters, Simulations, and Secular Evolution"
**Concepts:** [[disk-galaxy-structure]], [[bulge-disk-decomposition]]

**Supports:**
- Bulge and disk structural parameters for disk-dominated galaxies, with simulations used to
  quantify how reliably the fit recovers them.

**Use when:**
- Citing the reliability of bulge parameters in a disk-dominated system.

**Do not use for:**
- Early-type galaxies.

## Fisher 2008 — classical bulges versus pseudobulges

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Fisher2008`
**Reference:** arXiv:0805.4206, AJ 136, 773 — "The Structure of Classical Bulges And
Pseudobulges: The Link Between Pseudobulges And Sérsic Index"
**Concepts:** [[disk-galaxy-structure]], [[bulge-disk-decomposition]], [[sersic-profile]]

**Supports:**
- Pseudobulges and classical bulges separate in Sersic index, with pseudobulges having low
  indices (near-exponential) and classical bulges higher ones.

**Use when:**
- Interpreting a fitted bulge Sersic index as evidence for bulge *type*.

**Do not use for:**
- Treating the index cut as a sharp, reliable classifier for an individual galaxy — it is a
  population statement.

## Kormendy 2004 — secular evolution and pseudobulges

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Kormendy2004`
**Reference:** arXiv:astro-ph/0407343, ARA&A 42, 603 — "Secular Evolution and the Formation
of Pseudobulges in Disk Galaxies"
**Concepts:** [[disk-galaxy-structure]], [[bulge-disk-decomposition]],
[[morphology-classification]]

**Supports:**
- The physical distinction between merger-built classical bulges and secularly built
  pseudobulges, and the observational criteria that separate them.

**Use when:**
- Explaining *why* a bulge's structural parameters carry formation information.

**Do not use for:**
- A quantitative structural catalogue.
- Note: a near-identical arXiv ID (astro-ph/0407434) is a different Kormendy conference
  paper, not this review.

## Laurikainen 2010 — photometric scaling relations of lenticulars and spirals

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Laurikainen2010`
**Reference:** arXiv:1002.4370, MNRAS 405, 1089 — "Photometric scaling relations of
lenticular and spiral galaxies" (ADS: 2010MNRAS.405.1089L)
**Concepts:** [[disk-galaxy-structure]], [[galaxy-scaling-relations]]

**Supports:**
- Multi-component near-infrared decompositions of lenticular and spiral galaxies and the
  scaling relations their bulges and disks follow.

**Use when:**
- Comparing a fitted S0 or spiral bulge against a near-infrared reference sample.

**Do not use for:**
- Optical-band conclusions without accounting for the wavelength difference.

## Pohlen 2006 — disk breaks in late-type spirals

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Pohlen2006`
**Reference:** arXiv:astro-ph/0603682, A&A 454, 759 — "The structure of galactic disks:
Studying late-type spiral galaxies using SDSS"
**Concepts:** [[disk-galaxy-structure]], [[sky-subtraction-and-photometry]]

**Supports:**
- Outer disk profiles are frequently *not* a single exponential: they break, either down
  (truncation) or up (anti-truncation), at a measurable radius.
- Classification of disk profiles into single-exponential, down-bending and up-bending types.

**Use when:**
- Deciding whether a single exponential disk is adequate out to the mask edge.

**Do not use for:**
- Early-type galaxies.

## Erwin 2008 — outer disks of early-type (barred) disk galaxies

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Erwin2008`
**Reference:** arXiv:0709.3505, AJ 135, 20 — "The Outer Disks of Early-Type Galaxies. I.
Surface-Brightness Profiles of Barred Galaxies"
**Concepts:** [[disk-galaxy-structure]]

**Supports:**
- Outer-disk profile types in early-type barred disk galaxies, extending break/anti-truncation
  classification beyond late types.

**Use when:**
- Fitting a barred S0/early-type disk and needing a precedent for a broken profile.

**Do not use for:**
- Unbarred late-type spirals.

## Sheth 2010 — S4G survey

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Sheth2010`
**Reference:** arXiv:1010.1592, PASP 122, 1397 — "The Spitzer Survey of Stellar Structure in
Galaxies (S4G)"
**Concepts:** [[disk-galaxy-structure]], [[stellar-mass-estimates]]

**Supports:**
- A mid-infrared imaging survey of thousands of nearby galaxies designed specifically for
  stellar structure, where the 3.6 micron light closely traces stellar mass.

**Use when:**
- Arguing for a near/mid-infrared band as the closest thing to a stellar-mass image.

**Do not use for:**
- High-resolution structural work — Spitzer's PSF is broad.

## Salo 2015 — S4G multi-component decompositions

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Salo2015`
**Reference:** arXiv:1503.06550, ApJS 219, 4 — "Spitzer Survey of Stellar Structure in
Galaxies (S4G). The Pipeline 4: Multi-component decomposition strategies and data release"
**Concepts:** [[disk-galaxy-structure]], [[bulge-disk-decomposition]]

**Supports:**
- A public multi-component decomposition catalogue for the S4G sample, with an explicit
  strategy for deciding how many components to fit.

**Use when:**
- You need a documented decision procedure for component count, or a mid-infrared structural
  reference sample.

**Do not use for:**
- Optical structural parameters.
