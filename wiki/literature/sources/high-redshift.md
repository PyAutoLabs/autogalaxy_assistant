# Sources: high-redshift galaxy structure

Measuring structure at high redshift is the same fit under worse conditions: fewer photons,
a PSF comparable to the galaxy, and — before JWST — the wrong rest-frame wavelength. These
are the catalogues, the size-evolution measurements, and the JWST results that changed the
picture.

## van der Wel 2012 — CANDELS structural parameters

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `vanderWel2012`
**Reference:** arXiv:1211.6954, ApJS 203, 24, doi:10.1088/0067-0049/203/2/24 — "Structural
Parameters of Galaxies in CANDELS"
**Concepts:** [[high-z-galaxy-structure]], [[candels]], [[photometric-structural-fitting]]

**Supports:**
- A uniform catalogue of Sersic-fit structural parameters for CANDELS galaxies, with
  simulation-based assessment of the random and systematic errors.

**Use when:**
- Citing the standard high-redshift structural catalogue, or the error budget of a Sersic
  fit on faint HST data.

**Do not use for:**
- The size–mass relation itself — that is the 2014 paper.

## van der Wel 2014 — the size-mass distribution since z = 3

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `vanderWel2014`
**Reference:** arXiv:1404.2844, ApJ 788, 28, doi:10.1088/0004-637X/788/1/28 —
"3D-HST+CANDELS: The Evolution of the Galaxy Size-Mass Distribution since z=3"
**Concepts:** [[high-z-galaxy-structure]], [[galaxy-scaling-relations]]

**Supports:**
- The size–mass relation and its evolution from z = 3 to the present, measured separately
  for star-forming and quiescent galaxies.
- Quiescent galaxies at fixed stellar mass were substantially smaller in the past than
  star-forming galaxies of the same mass.

**Use when:**
- Placing a measured effective radius in the context of the population at that redshift and
  mass. This is the standard reference relation.

**Do not use for:**
- Rest-frame near-infrared sizes — the measurements are HST-era.

## Wuyts 2011 — structure across the star-formation main sequence

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Wuyts2011`
**Reference:** arXiv:1107.0317, ApJ 742, 96 — "Galaxy Structure and Mode of Star Formation
in the SFR-Mass Plane from z ~ 2.5 to z ~ 0.1"
**Concepts:** [[high-z-galaxy-structure]], [[morphology-classification]]

**Supports:**
- Sersic index and surface-brightness structure vary systematically across the star-formation
  rate–stellar mass plane, over 0.1 < z < 2.5.

**Use when:**
- Connecting a structural measurement to a galaxy's star-formation state.

**Do not use for:**
- Causal claims about quenching.

## Suess 2019 — colour gradients and half-mass radii

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Suess2019`
**Reference:** arXiv:1904.10992, ApJ 877, 103, doi:10.3847/1538-4357/ab1bda — "Half-mass
Radii for ~7000 Galaxies at 1.0 <= z <= 2.5: Most of the Evolution in the Mass-Size Relation
Is Due to Color Gradients"
**Concepts:** [[high-z-galaxy-structure]], [[stellar-mass-estimates]],
[[galaxy-scaling-relations]]

**Supports:**
- Half-*mass* radii differ systematically from half-*light* radii because of internal colour
  gradients.
- Much of the apparent evolution in the mass–size relation is attributable to those
  gradients rather than to structural growth.

**Use when:**
- Warning that a fitted effective radius is a light-weighted, band-dependent quantity.

**Do not use for:**
- Claiming there is no real size evolution — the paper reframes its magnitude, not its
  existence.

## Suess 2022 — rest-frame near-infrared sizes with JWST

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Suess2022`
**Reference:** arXiv:2207.10655, ApJL 937, L33, doi:10.3847/2041-8213/ac8e06 — "Rest-frame
Near-infrared Sizes of Galaxies at Cosmic Noon: Objects in JWST's Mirror Are Smaller than
They Appeared"
**Concepts:** [[high-z-galaxy-structure]], [[jwst]]

**Supports:**
- Sizes measured in the rest-frame near-infrared with JWST are systematically smaller than
  those measured at shorter rest-frame wavelengths with HST.

**Use when:**
- Explaining why a JWST long-wavelength size is not directly comparable to an HST-era size.

**Do not use for:**
- Local galaxies.

## Ferreira 2022 — first JWST rest-frame optical structure at z > 3

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Ferreira2022`
**Reference:** arXiv:2207.09428, ApJL 938, L2, doi:10.3847/2041-8213/ac947c — "Panic! At the
Disks: First Rest-frame Optical Observations of Galaxy Structure at z > 3 with JWST in the
SMACS 0723 Field"
**Concepts:** [[high-z-galaxy-structure]], [[morphology-classification]], [[jwst]]

**Supports:**
- Disk-like morphologies are far more common at z > 3 in rest-frame optical JWST imaging
  than pre-JWST rest-frame ultraviolet studies implied.

**Use when:**
- Making the morphological k-correction argument concrete.

**Do not use for:**
- A wide-area statistical claim — the field is SMACS 0723, not a survey.

## Kartaltepe 2023 — CEERS morphology at z = 3-9

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Kartaltepe2023`
**Reference:** arXiv:2210.14713, ApJL 946, L15, doi:10.3847/2041-8213/acad01 — "CEERS Key
Paper. III. The Diversity of Galaxy Structure and Morphology at z = 3-9 with JWST"
**Concepts:** [[high-z-galaxy-structure]], [[morphology-classification]], [[jwst]]

**Supports:**
- Structural and morphological diversity of galaxies over z = 3–9 measured from JWST
  imaging, including substantial disk and spheroid fractions.

**Use when:**
- Citing the JWST-era morphological mix at high redshift.

**Do not use for:**
- Parametric size measurements — the emphasis is classification.

## Robertson 2023 — machine-learning morphologies with JWST

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Robertson2023`
**Reference:** arXiv:2208.11456, ApJL 942, L42, doi:10.3847/2041-8213/aca086 — "Morpheus
Reveals Distant Disk Galaxy Morphologies with JWST: The First AI/ML Analysis of JWST Images"
**Concepts:** [[morphology-classification]], [[high-z-galaxy-structure]], [[jwst]]

**Supports:**
- Deep-learning morphological classification applied to JWST imaging, recovering disk
  morphologies at high redshift.

**Use when:**
- Citing automated classification as an alternative to parametric fitting at survey scale.

**Do not use for:**
- Structural parameters such as effective radius or Sersic index.

## Elmegreen 2007 — resolved clumpy disks in the UDF

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Elmegreen2007`
**Reference:** arXiv:astro-ph/0701121, ApJ 658, 763 — "Resolved Galaxies in the Hubble Ultra
Deep Field: Star Formation in Disks at High Redshift"
**Concepts:** [[clumpy-and-irregular-structure]], [[high-z-galaxy-structure]]

**Supports:**
- High-redshift disks are frequently resolved into a small number of giant star-forming
  clumps rather than a smooth exponential.

**Use when:**
- Explaining why a smooth parametric model can fit badly at high redshift.

**Do not use for:**
- Quantitative clump statistics over a large sample — cite Guo 2015.

## Guo 2015 — clumpy galaxies in CANDELS

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Guo2015`
**Reference:** arXiv:1410.7398, ApJ 800, 39 — "Clumpy Galaxies in CANDELS. I. The Definition
of UV Clumps and the Fraction of Clumpy Galaxies at 0.5<z<3"
**Concepts:** [[clumpy-and-irregular-structure]], [[candels]]

**Supports:**
- A quantitative definition of a UV clump and the fraction of galaxies that are clumpy over
  0.5 < z < 3.

**Use when:**
- Deciding whether clumpiness is expected for a galaxy at a given redshift and mass.

**Do not use for:**
- Rest-frame optical clump properties.

## Foerster Schreiber 2011 — kiloparsec-scale clumps at z ~ 2

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `ForsterSchreiber2011`
**Reference:** arXiv:1104.0248, ApJ 739, 45, doi:10.1088/0004-637X/739/1/45 — "Constraints on
the Assembly and Dynamics of Galaxies. II. Properties of Kiloparsec-scale Clumps in
Rest-frame Optical Emission of z ~ 2 Star-forming Galaxies"
**Concepts:** [[clumpy-and-irregular-structure]], [[high-z-galaxy-structure]]

**Supports:**
- Properties (sizes, masses, luminosities) of kpc-scale clumps measured in the rest-frame
  optical at z ~ 2.

**Use when:**
- Arguing that clumps persist in the rest-frame optical and are not purely a UV effect.

**Do not use for:**
- Clump lifetimes, which the paper constrains only indirectly.

## Bruce 2012 — morphologies of massive galaxies at 1 < z < 3

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Bruce2012`
**Reference:** arXiv:1206.4322, MNRAS 427, 1666 — "The Morphologies of Massive Galaxies at
1<z<3 in the CANDELS-UDS Field: Compact Bulges, and the Rise and Fall of Massive Disks"
**Concepts:** [[high-z-galaxy-structure]], [[bulge-disk-decomposition]], [[candels]]

**Supports:**
- Massive galaxies at 1 < z < 3 frequently contain both a compact bulge and a disk
  component, with the disk fraction evolving over that interval.

**Use when:**
- Motivating a two-component model at high redshift rather than a single Sersic.

**Do not use for:**
- Low-mass galaxies.
