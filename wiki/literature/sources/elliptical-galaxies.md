# Sources: elliptical and early-type galaxy structure

What early-type galaxies actually look like when you resolve them: not one family, but
several, separated by isophotal shape, nuclear profile and rotation.

## Caon 1993 — the shape of early-type light profiles

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Caon1993`
**Reference:** Caon, Capaccioli & D'Onofrio 1993, MNRAS 265, 1013 — "On the shape of the
light profiles of early-type galaxies"
**Concepts:** [[early-type-galaxy-structure]], [[sersic-profile]]

**Supports:**
- Early-type galaxy profiles are better described by a free Sersic index than by a fixed
  R^(1/4) law.
- The best-fitting Sersic index correlates with galaxy size and luminosity.

**Use when:**
- Justifying a free Sersic index for an early-type galaxy instead of fixing n = 4.

**Do not use for:**
- Disk galaxies.

## Kormendy 2009 — structure and formation of ellipticals and spheroidals

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Kormendy2009`
**Reference:** arXiv:0810.1681, ApJS 182, 216 — "Structure and Formation of Elliptical and
Spheroidal Galaxies"
**Concepts:** [[early-type-galaxy-structure]], [[sersic-profile]], [[isophote-analysis]]

**Supports:**
- A comprehensive photometric analysis of Virgo early-type galaxies, with Sersic fits over
  large radial ranges.
- The separation between "core" ellipticals (light deficits relative to the inward
  extrapolation of the Sersic fit) and "coreless"/extra-light ellipticals.
- Ellipticals and spheroidal galaxies are structurally distinct families rather than a
  continuous sequence.

**Use when:**
- Interpreting an inner deviation from a Sersic fit, or citing the reference photometric
  analysis of nearby early types.

**Do not use for:**
- High-redshift systems.

## Faber 1997 — central parameter relations

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Faber1997`
**Reference:** arXiv:astro-ph/9610055, AJ 114, 1771 — "The Centers of Early-Type Galaxies
with HST. IV. Central Parameter Relations"
**Concepts:** [[early-type-galaxy-structure]]

**Supports:**
- HST imaging separates early-type nuclear profiles into cores and power-laws, and the two
  classes occupy different regions of the luminosity–central-structure plane.

**Use when:**
- Citing the observational basis of the core/power-law dichotomy.

**Do not use for:**
- The updated bimodality analysis — cite Lauer 2007.

## Lauer 2007 — bimodal central surface-brightness profiles

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Lauer2007`
**Reference:** arXiv:astro-ph/0609762, ApJ 664, 226 — "The Centers of Early-Type Galaxies with
Hubble Space Telescope. VI. Bimodal Central Surface Brightness Profiles"
**Concepts:** [[early-type-galaxy-structure]], [[isophote-analysis]]

**Supports:**
- The distribution of inner profile slopes in early-type galaxies is bimodal, supporting a
  genuine core/cusp division rather than a continuum.

**Use when:**
- Deciding whether a central light deficit in a fit is expected for a galaxy of that
  luminosity.

**Do not use for:**
- Galaxies whose nuclei are unresolved in your data.

## Cote 2006 — nuclei of early-type galaxies

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Cote2006`
**Reference:** arXiv:astro-ph/0603252, ApJS 165, 57, doi:10.1086/504042 — "The ACS Virgo
Cluster Survey. VIII. The Nuclei of Early-Type Galaxies"
**Concepts:** [[early-type-galaxy-structure]], [[point-spread-function]]

**Supports:**
- Compact stellar nuclei are common in early-type galaxies, particularly at intermediate
  luminosities.

**Use when:**
- Deciding whether a central point-like excess in residuals is a nucleus rather than a
  fitting artefact.

**Do not use for:**
- Nuclear black-hole masses.

## Hopkins 2009 — dissipation and extra light

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Hopkins2009`
**Reference:** arXiv:0805.3533, ApJS 181, 135 — "Dissipation and Extra Light in Galactic
Nuclei: II. 'Cusp' Ellipticals"
**Concepts:** [[early-type-galaxy-structure]], [[bulge-disk-decomposition]]

**Supports:**
- Cusp ellipticals are naturally described as an outer violently relaxed component plus an
  inner dissipational ("extra light") component, so a two-component fit has a physical
  interpretation.

**Use when:**
- Motivating a two-component decomposition of an elliptical galaxy.

**Do not use for:**
- Core ellipticals (Paper III) or gas-rich merger remnants (Paper I) — this is Paper II of a
  multi-part series.

## Huang 2013 — three-component structure of nearby ellipticals

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Huang2013`
**Reference:** arXiv:1212.2639, ApJ 766, 47 — "The Carnegie-Irvine Galaxy Survey. III. The
Three-Component Structure of Nearby Elliptical Galaxies"
**Concepts:** [[early-type-galaxy-structure]], [[bulge-disk-decomposition]],
[[multi-gaussian-expansion]]

**Supports:**
- Nearby elliptical galaxies are frequently better fitted by three Sersic components than by
  one, with the components spanning distinct radial ranges.

**Use when:**
- Justifying more than one component for a galaxy that is morphologically a single
  elliptical.

**Do not use for:**
- A claim that the three components are dynamically distinct populations.

## Emsellem 2011 — fast and slow rotators

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Emsellem2011`
**Reference:** arXiv:1102.4444, MNRAS 414, 888 — "The ATLAS3D project - III. A census of the
stellar angular momentum within the effective radius of early-type galaxies: unveiling the
distribution of Fast and Slow Rotators"
**Concepts:** [[early-type-galaxy-structure]], [[morphology-classification]]

**Supports:**
- Early-type galaxies divide kinematically into fast and slow rotators, and this division
  does not map cleanly onto the photometric E/S0 classification.

**Use when:**
- Cautioning that a photometric early-type classification does not determine the dynamical
  class.

**Do not use for:**
- Photometric structural parameters.

## Cappellari 2016 — the IFS view of early-type structure

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Cappellari2016`
**Reference:** arXiv:1602.04267, ARA&A 54, 597 — "Structure and Kinematics of Early-Type
Galaxies from Integral-Field Spectroscopy"
**Concepts:** [[early-type-galaxy-structure]], [[galaxy-scaling-relations]]

**Supports:**
- A synthesis of what integral-field spectroscopy established about early-type galaxy
  structure, kinematics and their scaling relations.

**Use when:**
- You need one authoritative review to anchor an early-type structural discussion.

**Do not use for:**
- Photometric fitting methodology.

## Graham 2013 — review of galaxy structure and scaling laws

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Graham2013`
**Reference:** arXiv:1108.0997 — "A Review of Elliptical and Disc Galaxy Structure, and
Modern Scaling Laws"; published as a chapter in *Planets, Stars and Stellar Systems* Vol. 6,
pp. 91–140 (Springer, 2013)
**Concepts:** [[early-type-galaxy-structure]], [[galaxy-scaling-relations]],
[[sersic-profile]]

**Supports:**
- A review of elliptical and disk galaxy structure, the Sersic model and the scaling
  relations that follow from it, including curvature in relations built on the R^(1/4)
  assumption.

**Use when:**
- You want a single pedagogical reference covering profile shape and scaling relations
  together.

**Do not use for:**
- Recent (post-2013) results.
