# Sources: surveys, missions and instruments

The facilities and programmes that produce the images a structural fit is run on. Cite these
for provenance — what the data are, how deep, at what resolution — not for scientific
results.

## York 2000 — the Sloan Digital Sky Survey

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `York2000`
**Reference:** arXiv:astro-ph/0006396, AJ 120, 1579 — "The Sloan Digital Sky Survey: Technical
Summary"
**Concepts:** [[sdss]]

**Supports:**
- The design of SDSS: imaging in five bands plus spectroscopy over a large sky area.

**Use when:**
- Citing SDSS as the source of imaging or redshifts.

**Do not use for:**
- Later SDSS phases — cite the relevant data release.

## Blanton 2017 — SDSS-IV

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Blanton2017`
**Reference:** arXiv:1703.00052, AJ 154, 28 — "Sloan Digital Sky Survey IV: Mapping the Milky
Way, Nearby Galaxies, and the Distant Universe"
**Concepts:** [[sdss]], [[manga]]

**Supports:**
- The scope of SDSS-IV, the phase that contains the MaNGA integral-field survey.

**Use when:**
- Citing the survey programme MaNGA sits inside.

**Do not use for:**
- Data products — cite the data release.

## Abdurro'uf 2022 — SDSS Data Release 17

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Abdurrouf2022`
**Reference:** arXiv:2112.02026, ApJS 259, 35, doi:10.3847/1538-4365/ac4414 — "The Seventeenth
Data Release of the Sloan Digital Sky Surveys: Complete Release of MaNGA, MaStar and APOGEE-2
Data"
**Concepts:** [[sdss]], [[manga]]

**Supports:**
- The final MaNGA data release and the complete SDSS-IV dataset.

**Use when:**
- Using SDSS DR17 data products.

**Do not use for:**
- Survey design.

## Driver 2011 — GAMA

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Driver2011`
**Reference:** arXiv:1009.0614, MNRAS 413, 971 — "Galaxy and Mass Assembly (GAMA): survey
diagnostics and core data release"
**Concepts:** [[galaxy-scaling-relations]], [[stellar-mass-estimates]]

**Supports:**
- A deep, highly complete spectroscopic survey over well-defined equatorial fields, with the
  ancillary imaging used for structural and mass–size studies.

**Use when:**
- Citing GAMA as the parent sample for a structural or scaling-relation measurement.

**Do not use for:**
- Imaging resolution comparable to space-based data.

## Grogin 2011 — CANDELS

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Grogin2011`
**Reference:** arXiv:1105.3753 — "CANDELS: The Cosmic Assembly Near-infrared Deep
Extragalactic Legacy Survey"
**Concepts:** [[candels]], [[hst]]

**Supports:**
- The design of the largest HST programme: WFC3/IR plus ACS imaging over five extragalactic
  fields in a deep and a wide tier.

**Use when:**
- Citing the CANDELS survey.

**Do not use for:**
- The mosaics themselves — cite Koekemoer 2011.

## Koekemoer 2011 — CANDELS data products

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Koekemoer2011`
**Reference:** arXiv:1105.3754 — "CANDELS: The Cosmic Assembly Near-infrared Deep
Extragalactic Legacy Survey - The Hubble Space Telescope Observations, Imaging Data Products
and Mosaics"
**Concepts:** [[candels]], [[hst]], [[sky-subtraction-and-photometry]]

**Supports:**
- The observations, calibration and mosaic construction behind the public CANDELS imaging.

**Use when:**
- Citing the provenance of CANDELS images used in a fit.

**Do not use for:**
- Survey rationale.

## Giavalisco 2004 — GOODS

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Giavalisco2004`
**Reference:** arXiv:astro-ph/0309105, ApJL 600, L93 — "The Great Observatories Origins Deep
Survey: Initial Results from Optical and Near-Infrared Imaging"
**Concepts:** [[hst]], [[high-z-galaxy-structure]]

**Supports:**
- The GOODS fields and their initial deep optical/near-infrared imaging.

**Use when:**
- Citing GOODS as the field a dataset comes from.

**Do not use for:**
- Later, deeper data in the same fields.

## Beckwith 2006 — the Hubble Ultra Deep Field

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Beckwith2006`
**Reference:** arXiv:astro-ph/0607632, AJ 132, 1729, doi:10.1086/507302 — "The Hubble Ultra
Deep Field"
**Concepts:** [[hst]], [[high-z-galaxy-structure]]

**Supports:**
- The deepest HST optical imaging of its era and its construction.

**Use when:**
- Citing UDF imaging, or the depth regime in which faint high-redshift structure is
  measurable.

**Do not use for:**
- Near-infrared depths, which came later.

## Aihara 2018 — Hyper Suprime-Cam SSP

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Aihara2018`
**Reference:** arXiv:1704.05858, PASJ 70, S4, doi:10.1093/pasj/psx066 — "The Hyper Suprime-Cam
SSP Survey: Overview and Survey Design"
**Concepts:** [[sky-subtraction-and-photometry]]

**Supports:**
- A wide-field, deep, good-seeing ground-based imaging survey — the ground-based dataset in
  which faint outer structure is most readily measured.

**Use when:**
- Citing HSC imaging, e.g. for outer-envelope measurements.

**Do not use for:**
- Space-based resolution claims.

## Ivezic 2019 — LSST

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Ivezic2019`
**Reference:** arXiv:0805.2366, ApJ 873, 111 — "LSST: From Science Drivers to Reference Design
and Anticipated Data Products"
**Concepts:** [[morphology-classification]]

**Supports:**
- The Rubin Observatory LSST design and its anticipated data products.

**Use when:**
- Motivating scalable, automated structural measurement for the coming decade.

**Do not use for:**
- Delivered data.

## Gardner 2023 — the JWST mission

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Gardner2023`
**Reference:** arXiv:2304.04869, PASP 135, 068001 — "The James Webb Space Telescope Mission"
**Concepts:** [[jwst]]

**Supports:**
- The JWST mission, its instruments and its as-built capabilities.

**Use when:**
- Citing JWST itself. (The 2006 pre-launch mission paper, arXiv:astro-ph/0606175, Space
  Science Reviews 123, 485, is the design-era reference.)

**Do not use for:**
- Measured on-orbit performance — cite Rigby 2023.

## Rigby 2023 — JWST science performance in commissioning

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Rigby2023`
**Reference:** arXiv:2207.05632, PASP 135, 048001 — "The Science Performance of JWST as
Characterized in Commissioning"
**Concepts:** [[jwst]], [[point-spread-function]]

**Supports:**
- The measured on-orbit performance of the observatory, including image quality, against
  pre-launch requirements.

**Use when:**
- Justifying the resolution or sensitivity assumed for a JWST dataset.

**Do not use for:**
- Instrument-specific detail — cite the instrument paper.

## Rieke 2023 — NIRCam in flight

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Rieke2023`
**Reference:** arXiv:2212.12069, PASP 135, 028001, doi:10.1088/1538-3873/acac53 — "NIRCam
Performance on JWST In Flight"
**Concepts:** [[jwst]], [[point-spread-function]]

**Supports:**
- NIRCam's in-flight performance, including its short- and long-wavelength channels and their
  imaging characteristics.

**Use when:**
- Citing the instrument that produced a NIRCam dataset — including the bundled COSMOS-Web
  cutout — or motivating a careful PSF treatment across its two channels.

**Do not use for:**
- Data-reduction pipeline behaviour.

## Sirianni 2005 — HST ACS photometric calibration

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Sirianni2005`
**Reference:** arXiv:astro-ph/0507614, PASP 117, 1049, doi:10.1086/444553 — "The Photometric
Performance and Calibration of the Hubble Space Telescope Advanced Camera for Surveys"
**Concepts:** [[hst]], [[sky-subtraction-and-photometry]]

**Supports:**
- ACS photometric performance, zeropoints and calibration — the basis for converting ACS
  counts into physical surface brightness.

**Use when:**
- Working with ACS data and needing the calibration reference.

**Do not use for:**
- WFC3 data.

## Kimble 2008 — HST Wide Field Camera 3

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Kimble2008`

Field Camera 3: a powerful new imager for the Hubble Space Telescope"
**Concepts:** [[hst]]

**Supports:**
- The WFC3 instrument, whose IR channel enabled rest-frame optical imaging at z ~ 2 and
  therefore CANDELS.

**Use when:**
- Citing the instrument behind WFC3/IR structural measurements.

**Do not use for:**

## Bretonniere 2022 — galaxy morphology forecasts

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Bretonniere2022`
**Reference:** arXiv:2105.12149, A&A 657, A90, doi:10.1051/0004-6361/202141393 — "Euclid

[[photometric-structural-fitting]]

**Supports:**
- Forecasts for how well galaxy morphology and structural parameters can be recovered from
  Euclid imaging, using realistic simulated galaxies.

**Use when:**

**Do not use for:**
- Delivered measurements.

## Walmsley 2025 — the Q1 visual morphology catalogue

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Walmsley2025`
**Reference:** arXiv:2503.15310 — "Euclid Quick Data Release (Q1): First visual morphology
catalogue" (Euclid Collaboration: Walmsley and others)
**Concepts:** [[euclid]], [[morphology-classification]]

- The first Euclid morphology catalogue, built from the Q1 data release.

**Use when:**
- Citing Euclid's first delivered morphological measurements.
