# Sources: galaxy scaling relations

The relations a structural measurement gets compared against — size–mass, Kormendy,
Faber–Jackson, the fundamental plane — plus the stellar-mass estimates that put a galaxy on
them. Every one of these relations inherits the systematics of the profile fit behind it.

## Faber 1976 — the Faber-Jackson relation

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Faber1976`
**Reference:** Faber & Jackson 1976, ApJ 204, 668, doi:10.1086/154215 — "Velocity dispersions
and mass-to-light ratios for elliptical galaxies"
**Concepts:** [[galaxy-scaling-relations]], [[early-type-galaxy-structure]]

**Supports:**
- Elliptical galaxy luminosity correlates with central stellar velocity dispersion.

**Use when:**
- Citing the origin of the luminosity–dispersion relation.

**Do not use for:**
- Modern calibrations.

## Kormendy 1977 — the Kormendy relation

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Kormendy1977`
**Reference:** Kormendy 1977, ApJ 218, 333 — "Brightness distributions in compact and normal
galaxies. II. Structure parameters of the spheroidal component" (ADS: 1977ApJ...218..333K)
**Concepts:** [[galaxy-scaling-relations]], [[early-type-galaxy-structure]]

**Supports:**
- Effective radius correlates with mean effective surface brightness for spheroidal
  components.

**Use when:**
- Placing a fitted effective radius and surface brightness on the classical relation.

**Do not use for:**
- Disk galaxies.

## Djorgovski 1987 — the fundamental plane

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Djorgovski1987`
**Reference:** Djorgovski & Davis 1987, ApJ 313, 59 — "Fundamental properties of elliptical
galaxies" (ADS: 1987ApJ...313...59D)
**Concepts:** [[galaxy-scaling-relations]], [[early-type-galaxy-structure]]

**Supports:**
- Elliptical galaxies occupy a two-dimensional plane in the space of effective radius,
  surface brightness and velocity dispersion.

**Use when:**
- Citing the fundamental plane. Pair with Dressler 1987, the independent discovery.

**Do not use for:**
- The plane's tilt or its physical interpretation.

## Dressler 1987 — the D_n-sigma distance estimator

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Dressler1987`
**Reference:** Dressler and others 1987, ApJ 313, 42 — "Spectroscopy and Photometry of
Elliptical Galaxies. I. New Distance Estimator" (ADS: 1987ApJ...313...42D)
**Concepts:** [[galaxy-scaling-relations]]

**Supports:**
- A photometric-plus-dispersion distance estimator for elliptical galaxies, the companion
  discovery to the fundamental plane.

**Use when:**
- Citing the fundamental plane's independent discovery.

**Do not use for:**
- Modern distance measurements.

## Shen 2003 — the SDSS size distribution

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Shen2003`
**Reference:** arXiv:astro-ph/0301527, MNRAS 343, 978 — "The size distribution of galaxies in
the Sloan Digital Sky Survey"
**Concepts:** [[galaxy-scaling-relations]], [[sdss]]

**Supports:**
- The distribution of galaxy sizes as a function of luminosity and stellar mass, measured
  separately for early- and late-type galaxies, with a characteristic log-normal scatter.

**Use when:**
- Citing the local size–mass relation and its intrinsic scatter.

**Do not use for:**
- Sizes measured from deeper or higher-resolution data — SDSS seeing limits the small end.

## Bernardi 2003 — the SDSS fundamental plane

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Bernardi2003`
**Reference:** arXiv:astro-ph/0301626, AJ 125, 1866 — "Early-type galaxies in the SDSS. III.
The Fundamental Plane"
**Concepts:** [[galaxy-scaling-relations]], [[early-type-galaxy-structure]]

**Supports:**
- The fundamental plane measured for a large, homogeneous SDSS early-type sample.

**Use when:**
- You need a large-sample local calibration of the plane.

**Do not use for:**
- Papers I, II, IV or V of the same series, which cover different content.

## Lange 2015 — GAMA mass-size relations by type

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Lange2015`
**Reference:** arXiv:1411.6355, MNRAS 447, 2603 — "Galaxy And Mass Assembly (GAMA): mass-size
relations of z<0.1 galaxies subdivided by Sérsic index, colour and morphology"
**Concepts:** [[galaxy-scaling-relations]], [[morphology-classification]]

**Supports:**
- The local mass–size relation depends strongly on how the sample is subdivided — by Sersic
  index, by colour, or by visual morphology — and these subdivisions do not agree.

**Use when:**
- Choosing which local relation to compare a measurement against, and justifying that choice.

**Do not use for:**
- z > 0.1.

## van der Wel 2014 — the size-mass relation to z = 3

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `vanderWel2014`
**Reference:** arXiv:1404.2844, ApJ 788, 28 — "3D-HST+CANDELS: The Evolution of the Galaxy
Size-Mass Distribution since z=3"
**Concepts:** [[galaxy-scaling-relations]], [[high-z-galaxy-structure]]

**Supports:**
- The size–mass relation and its evolution to z = 3 for star-forming and quiescent galaxies
  separately.

**Use when:**
- Comparing a high-redshift size measurement against the standard relation.

**Do not use for:**
- Half-mass (as opposed to half-light) sizes.

## Bell 2001 — stellar mass-to-light ratios

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Bell2001`
**Reference:** arXiv:astro-ph/0011493, ApJ 550, 212 — "Stellar mass-to-light ratios and the
Tully-Fisher relation"
**Concepts:** [[stellar-mass-estimates]], [[galaxy-scaling-relations]]

**Supports:**
- Stellar mass-to-light ratio correlates with broadband colour, giving the colour–M/L
  relations used to convert a fitted flux into a stellar mass.

**Use when:**
- Converting the luminosity of a fitted component into a stellar mass without full SED
  fitting.

**Do not use for:**
- High-precision masses, where the IMF and star-formation-history assumptions dominate.

## Kauffmann 2003 — SDSS stellar masses and star-formation histories

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Kauffmann2003`
**Reference:** arXiv:astro-ph/0204055, MNRAS 341, 33 — "Stellar Masses and Star Formation
Histories for 10^5 Galaxies from the Sloan Digital Sky Survey"
**Concepts:** [[stellar-mass-estimates]]

**Supports:**
- Spectroscopically derived stellar masses and star-formation histories for a large SDSS
  sample.

**Use when:**
- Citing the standard local stellar-mass catalogue used alongside structural parameters.

**Do not use for:**
- Photometric-only mass estimates at high redshift.

## Taylor 2011 — GAMA stellar mass estimates

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Taylor2011`
**Reference:** arXiv:1108.0635, MNRAS 418, 1587 — "Galaxy And Mass Assembly (GAMA): stellar
mass estimates"
**Concepts:** [[stellar-mass-estimates]]

**Supports:**
- Stellar-mass estimates for the GAMA sample and an assessment of their systematic
  uncertainties.

**Use when:**
- You need a documented uncertainty budget for a photometric stellar mass.

**Do not use for:**
- Resolved (per-pixel) mass maps.

## Conroy 2013 — modelling galaxy SEDs

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Conroy2013`
**Reference:** arXiv:1301.7095, ARA&A 51, 393 — "Modeling the Panchromatic Spectral Energy
Distributions of Galaxies"
**Concepts:** [[stellar-mass-estimates]]

**Supports:**
- A review of stellar-population synthesis and the dominant systematic uncertainties — IMF,
  stellar evolution phases, dust — in stellar masses derived from photometry.

**Use when:**
- Stating how uncertain a stellar mass really is when quoting a mass–size point.

**Do not use for:**
- Structural measurement.

## Bernardi 2013 — profile choice moves the mass function

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Bernardi2013`
**Reference:** arXiv:1304.7778, doi:10.1093/mnras/stt1607 — "The massive end of the luminosity
and stellar mass functions: Dependence on the fit to the light profile"
**Concepts:** [[galaxy-scaling-relations]], [[stellar-mass-estimates]]

**Supports:**
- The high-mass end of the stellar-mass function depends on which light profile was fitted.

**Use when:**
- Warning that a scaling relation inherits the systematics of the structural fit behind it.

**Do not use for:**
- The low-mass end.
