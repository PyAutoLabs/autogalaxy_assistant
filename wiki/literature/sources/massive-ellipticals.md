# Sources: massive galaxies and size growth

The most-cited result in galaxy structure of the last twenty years: massive quiescent
galaxies were far more compact in the early universe than their descendants are now. These
are the measurements, the proposed mechanism, and the checks on whether the effect is real
rather than a measurement artefact.

## Trujillo 2007 — strong size evolution since z ~ 2

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Trujillo2007`
**Reference:** arXiv:0709.0621, MNRAS 382, 109 — "Strong size evolution of the most massive
galaxies since z~2"
**Concepts:** [[early-type-galaxy-structure]], [[high-z-galaxy-structure]],
[[galaxy-scaling-relations]]

**Supports:**
- Massive galaxies at z ~ 2 have effective radii several times smaller than local galaxies
  of the same stellar mass.

**Use when:**
- Citing the size-evolution result itself.

**Do not use for:**
- The mechanism responsible.

## Buitrago 2008 — size evolution at 1.7 < z < 3

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Buitrago2008`
**Reference:** arXiv:0807.4141, ApJ 687, L61, doi:10.1086/592836 — "Size Evolution of the Most
Massive Galaxies at 1.7 < z < 3 from GOODS NICMOS Survey Imaging"
**Concepts:** [[early-type-galaxy-structure]], [[high-z-galaxy-structure]]

**Supports:**
- Near-infrared (rest-frame optical) imaging confirms compact sizes for massive galaxies at
  1.7 < z < 3, so the effect is not a rest-frame-ultraviolet artefact.

**Use when:**
- Rebutting the wavelength-artefact objection to size evolution.

**Do not use for:**
- Low-mass galaxies.

## Damjanov 2009 — red nuggets

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Damjanov2009`
**Reference:** arXiv:0807.1744, ApJ 695, 101, doi:10.1088/0004-637X/695/1/101 — "Red Nuggets
at z~1.5: Compact Passive Galaxies and the Formation of the Kormendy Relation"
**Concepts:** [[early-type-galaxy-structure]], [[galaxy-scaling-relations]]

**Supports:**
- Compact passive galaxies ("red nuggets") at z ~ 1.5 lie off the local Kormendy relation.

**Use when:**
- Naming the compact quiescent population, or connecting it to a local scaling relation.

**Do not use for:**
- The later, higher-redshift Damjanov paper's results (arXiv:1108.0656).

## Bezanson 2009 — compact high-z galaxies as the cores of local ellipticals

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Bezanson2009`
**Reference:** arXiv:0903.2044, ApJ 697, 1290 — "The Relation Between Compact, Quiescent High
Redshift Galaxies and Massive Nearby Elliptical Galaxies: Evidence for Hierarchical,
Inside-Out Growth"
**Concepts:** [[early-type-galaxy-structure]]

**Supports:**
- The central mass densities of compact z ~ 2 quiescent galaxies match the *inner* regions of
  local massive ellipticals, implying growth by adding material at large radii.

**Use when:**
- Explaining inside-out growth, or interpreting an outer envelope as accreted.

**Do not use for:**
- Claiming individual descendants have been identified.

## Naab 2009 — minor mergers as the size-growth mechanism

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Naab2009`
**Reference:** arXiv:0903.1636, ApJL 699, L178, doi:10.1088/0004-637X/699/2/L178 — "Minor
Mergers and the Size Evolution of Elliptical Galaxies"
**Concepts:** [[early-type-galaxy-structure]]

**Supports:**
- Minor (rather than major) mergers grow the size of an elliptical galaxy much faster than
  its mass, and are efficient enough to explain the observed evolution.

**Use when:**
- Citing the standard theoretical explanation for size growth.

**Do not use for:**
- An observational measurement.

## Oser 2010 — the two phases of galaxy formation

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Oser2010`
**Reference:** arXiv:1010.1381, ApJ 725, 2312 — "The Two Phases of Galaxy Formation"
**Concepts:** [[early-type-galaxy-structure]]

**Supports:**
- Massive galaxies form in two phases: early in-situ star formation, then late accretion of
  stars formed elsewhere, deposited preferentially at large radii.

**Use when:**
- Giving the theoretical framing for a compact core plus an extended envelope.

**Do not use for:**
- Observational profile measurements.

## van Dokkum 2010 — the growth of massive galaxies since z = 2

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `vanDokkum2010`
**Reference:** arXiv:0912.0514, ApJ 709, 1018 — "The Growth of Massive Galaxies Since z=2"
**Concepts:** [[early-type-galaxy-structure]], [[high-z-galaxy-structure]]

**Supports:**
- Following galaxies at fixed number density, mass growth since z = 2 occurs predominantly
  in the outer regions while the inner profile stays roughly fixed.

**Use when:**
- Citing direct profile-level evidence for inside-out growth.

**Do not use for:**
- Individual galaxy histories — the argument is statistical.

## Szomoru 2012 — profiles of quiescent galaxies at z ~ 2

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Szomoru2012`
**Reference:** arXiv:1111.3361, ApJ 749, 121, doi:10.1088/0004-637X/749/2/121 — "Sizes and
Surface Brightness Profiles of Quiescent Galaxies at z ~ 2"
**Concepts:** [[early-type-galaxy-structure]], [[photometric-structural-fitting]]

**Supports:**
- Residual-corrected surface-brightness profiles of z ~ 2 quiescent galaxies, testing whether
  the small measured sizes survive a careful treatment of the fit residuals and the PSF.

**Use when:**
- Answering "could the compact sizes be a fitting artefact?" with a methodological check.

**Do not use for:**
- Star-forming galaxies.

## Barro 2014 — compaction and compact star-forming progenitors

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Barro2014`
**Reference:** arXiv:1311.5559, ApJ 791, 52, doi:10.1088/0004-637X/791/1/52 — "CANDELS+3D-HST:
Compact SFGs at z~2-3, the Progenitors of the First Quiescent Galaxies"
**Concepts:** [[early-type-galaxy-structure]], [[high-z-galaxy-structure]]

**Supports:**
- A population of compact star-forming galaxies at z ~ 2–3 with the structural properties
  expected of the progenitors of compact quiescent galaxies.

**Use when:**
- Discussing how compact quiescent galaxies came to be compact in the first place.

**Do not use for:**
- Local galaxies.
